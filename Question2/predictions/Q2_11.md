# Q2.11: Predictions before training models B-D

Written after Testing the Baseline (Tables 3-4, Figures 3-5) and before training B, C, D or D+B.
Failures found for model A: small but nonzero consistency gaps (C_M = 0.00156, C_R = 0.00275, rotation
worse), and a speed failure that already starts at 2x (E(2) = 0.0439, above Blank) and grows at 3x
(0.0513), with digits stalling and blurring near their last observed position.

**Prediction.** Model B (symmetry augmentation) will shrink C_M and C_R several-fold and bring E_M and
E_R down to its E(5) at 1x, but will not change the speed failure. Model C (speed augmentation) will
largely fix 2x, where the motion (about 6.6 px per step) is still within model A's 8 px reach, but only
partly fix 3x (about 10 px per step, beyond that reach), and will not change the consistency gaps.
Model D (reach 16 px plus speed augmentation) will also fix 3x, bringing E(2) at 3x close to its value at
2x at a small cost in E(5) at 1x from the coarser 8x8 grid, while its consistency gaps stay nonzero like
those of A and C.
